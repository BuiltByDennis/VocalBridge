# Phase 18: Dataset Audit

## 1. Atypical English Datasets

### A. Euphonia / Speech Accessibility Project (SAP)
- **Language:** English (US)
- **Etiology:** ALS, Cerebral Palsy, Parkinson's, Down Syndrome.
- **Size:** Very Large.
- **Availability:** Restricted. Requires institutional data use agreement (DUA).
- **Commercial Use:** Blocked.
- **Relevance:** The gold standard, but legally inaccessible for Kasa Me production.

### B. Torgo Database
- **Language:** English (Canada)
- **Etiology:** Dysarthria (Cerebral Palsy, ALS).
- **Speakers:** 8 dysarthric, 7 control.
- **License:** Open (Research/Non-Commercial).
- **Commercial Use:** Blocked without permission.

## 2. Ghanaian Language Datasets (Re-evaluation)

### A. Common Voice (Akan/Dagbani/etc)
- **Language:** Akan, Dagbani, Ewe (Minimal)
- **Atypical Speech:** NONE.
- **License:** CC0 (Commercial permitted).
- **Size:** Very small for Twi (under 10 hours).

### B. FLEURS (Asante Twi)
- **Language:** Asante Twi
- **Atypical Speech:** NONE.
- **License:** CC-BY.
- **Size:** ~12 hours.

## 3. Conclusion
There are **zero** commercially-cleared datasets containing atypical Ghanaian-language speech. Any adaptation prototypes must rely on simulating atypical English using accessible datasets (like Torgo under research rules) and projecting those theories onto the Ghanaian context.
