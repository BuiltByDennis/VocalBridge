# Phase 18: Ghanaian Language + Atypical Speech Gap (Track C)

## 1. The Core Problem
Kasa Me's ultimate goal is to recognize atypical Ghanaian speech. However, Track C is currently an empty intersection.

## 2. Research Findings
1. **Data Availability:** **ZERO**. There is no publicly available dataset containing dysarthric or otherwise atypical speech for Akan, Ewe, Ga, Dagbani, or any other indigenous Ghanaian language.
2. **Transfer Learning Limits:** You cannot train an atypical English model and expect it to magically recognize typical Twi. You also cannot train a typical Twi model (e.g. on UGAkan) and expect it to recognize atypical Twi.
3. **The Unavoidable Requirement:** Before Kasa Me can claim to support Track C, a formal data collection effort must be undertaken in Ghana, subject to strict medical/ethical consent and HIPAA/data-protection equivalents, to collect dysarthric Twi/Ga/Ewe speech.

## 3. Conclusion
Track C is **BLOCKED BY DATA**. Any claims to the contrary are scientifically invalid. Future work must focus on Track A (Adapting English to atypical speech) or Track B (Baseline Ghanaian language ASR) independently before combining them.
