# Phase 16 Data Provenance

All controlled vocabulary entries introduced in Phase 16 are standard proper nouns (institutions, regions, cities) publicly available as general factual knowledge. No proprietary, copyrighted dictionaries or crawled parallel corpora were used.

## 1. Institutions Dictionary
- **Source**: General public knowledge (e.g. UMaT, KNUST, UG).
- **License**: Public Domain factual entity names.
- **Permitted Use**: Redistribution permitted.

## 2. Places Dictionary
- **Source**: Publicly recognized geopolitical regions and cities in Ghana.
- **License**: Public Domain factual entity names.
- **Permitted Use**: Redistribution permitted.

## 3. Expressions Dictionary
- **Source**: Generally established cultural loan words (waakye, trotro, dumsor, MoMo).
- **License**: Uncopyrightable standard nomenclature.
- **Permitted Use**: Redistribution permitted.

No raw acoustic data or crawled transcription data was bundled. The application remains 100% offline with deterministically baked-in dictionaries.
