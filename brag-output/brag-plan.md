# VocalBridge Brag Plan

## 1. What is the app?
VocalBridge (kasa_me) is a privacy-first, offline-capable personalized edge speech recognition app designed for people with atypical or impaired speech.

## 2. What is the funniest or most impressive claim?
"The system adapts to the speaker rather than forcing the speaker to adapt to the system."

## 3. What is the visual hook?
Accessible UI with high-contrast themes, large typography, and a massive motor-accessible Push-to-Talk button.

## 4. What should be shown from the actual UI?
The large Push-to-Talk button, followed by real-time speech transcription displaying on a high-contrast screen with Twi text.

## 5. What is the shortest satisfying video?
~18 seconds. 

## 6. What tone fits best?
Preset: `polished`
Direction: "Quiet, premium assistive technology film, focusing on empowerment and privacy, centered on Akan/Twi."

## 7. What should the audio feel like?
Warm corporate bed with subtle UI clicks for the push-to-talk button and text appearing. 
Music cue guidance: Use a calm, empowering, and premium audio track.

## 8. What should the share caption say?
VocalBridge is a new on-device speech recognition app that actually learns how you speak—no cloud required, and built for Akan/Twi.

## 9. What's the user flow worth showing?
Tap large Push-to-Talk button → Speak (atypical speech) → See real-time Twi transcription → System adapts locally.

## Storyboard

**Total duration:** ~18s

**Scene 1: The Hook (0:00 - 0:03)**
- **Visual:** High contrast black background. The phrase "Speech tech wasn't built for Twi." appears in large, elegant Chicago font (white text).
- **Motion:** Fade in smoothly, hold for 2s, cut.
- **Audio:** Music swells warmly. No SFX.

**Scene 2: The Reveal (0:03 - 0:06)**
- **Visual:** "So we built an offline AI that adapts to you." Text replaces previous.
- **Motion:** Fade in, hold 2s, slide up out of frame.
- **Audio:** Subtle whoosh SFX.

**Scene 3: UI - The Button (0:06 - 0:09)**
- **Visual:** The VocalBridge large Push-to-Talk button scales into view. It is pressed (visual state change). 
- **Motion:** Pop-in animation, press animation.
- **Audio:** Satisfying, deep UI click SFX.

**Scene 4: UI - Transcription (0:09 - 0:13)**
- **Visual:** The button shrinks down, making room for a large transcription box. Text types out: "Mehia me nnuru" (I need my medication).
- **Motion:** Quick slide transition, typewriter effect for text (fast-in, then hold).
- **Audio:** Subtle typing or popping SFX.

**Scene 5: The Flex (0:13 - 0:15)**
- **Visual:** A small badge appears below the text: "100% Offline. Akan/Twi Support."
- **Motion:** Badge pops in with a slight bounce.
- **Audio:** Premium chime or bell SFX.

**Scene 6: Outro (0:15 - 0:18)**
- **Visual:** The VocalBridge logo/name centers on screen. "VocalBridge" in large text.
- **Motion:** Fade in from black, hold.
- **Audio:** Final music chord rings out and fades.
